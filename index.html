01

Executive summary

Why this matters to the executive

What are the core challenges to scaling what currently exists?

The challenge is scaling the info from partners and sponsors that the Elevate festival receives every year (around 52 partners). In 2029, there will be two Elevate festivals, so it doubles the work for the Elevate team.

What pillar are you addressing?

Operational capacity

What opportunity does solving the problem bring?

  • Less work for the Partnerships and Sponsorship Manager (without adding additional staff for this task)
  • Lower probability of a mistake
  • Partners and sponsors get visibility without additional requests (for the second Elevate festival)

I am asking for approval of a one-year project: build it in 2027, test it at the 2028 festival and use it for both editions in 2029.

02

Current state

Where we are now

Brief description of the content system in relation to this pillar

The Partnerships and Sponsorship Manager asks the partners and sponsors to resubmit their information and logos once every year; in 2029, it will be twice per year.

Why it matters to the organization

The organization gets funding from partners and sponsors, so the Elevate team must make sure that partners get the visibility they paid for.

Current situation

52 partners and sponsors

The current website lists 52 partners and sponsors.

Primary pain points related to scaling

  • 52 partners and sponsors × 60 minutes (e-mail writing, downloading the logo, updating the website and other media; final check) = roughly 50 hours of work for one festival estimate
  • The festival team doesn't have a centralized folder for valid logos (and until when they are valid)
  • Risk of using outdated info
03

Scaling challenge

What problems will be solved

What scalability problem will you address?

Collecting the same info every year without checking on our side if it is still valid.

What happens if this isn't solved?

We get 50 hours of additional work estimate; partners and sponsors get the same email twice in 2029, and we lose our image as professionals.

What is the desired future state?

  • Getting the info once and adding a "valid until" date
  • The team asks partners and sponsors when something expires (and additionally once every two years)
  • For the second Elevate festival, part of the work will be done in the preparation phase for the first festival
04

Scope and objectives

What we want to achieve

What will you focus on?

Getting the info from partners and sponsors (name, logo, description).

What will you not address in this project?

Information from artists and speakers; info from new partners and sponsors.

Success criteria

How we'll know if we're successful

How will you know your scaling strategy works?

  • Test at the 2028 festival (count working hours, number of e-mails sent, errors found).
  • In 2029, partners will receive one request at most.
